## Founding principle: quality beats quantity (REQUIRED, not optional)

A result is valuable ONLY when: (a) business identity matches niche and location, (b) business activity has fresh evidence and no strong contradiction, (c) website category is correct, (d) contact belongs to that business, (e) email verification supports deliverability, (f) collected fields are export-licensed, and (g) no known suppression/closure flags. Do not show arbitrary scores like 97% if not trained/calibrated. Use **evidence-based status labels** and auditable deterministic ranking; do not invent probability of purchase. Never claim "verified active user" for an inbox or promise zero bounces.

**Separate five verification dimensions**:
1. `business_activity`: `LIKELY_ACTIVE`, `CLOSED`, `TEMPORARILY_CLOSED`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICTING`, with explicit source and timestamp. Consider Overture `operating_status`/existence confidence and `sources[].update_time`, current official web updates and authorized other independent evidence. If the dataset says `permanently_closed`, exclude from both lead finders by default. A governmental `active` registration says the legal entity may exist, not that the location is trading. Stale social posts, footer copyright years, page load, or `operating_status=open` alone are not definitive. Unknown or conflicting goes to review queue, not "verified operational" marketing. A site being down is NOT proof a business is closed.
2. `website_status`: `OWNED_SITE_FOUND`, `NO_OWNED_SITE_FOUND`, `SITE_UNREACHABLE`, `PARKED_OR_FOR_SALE`, `SOCIAL_ONLY`, `UNKNOWN`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Distinguish business-owned website from Facebook/Instagram/marketplace/directory listings. A `200` HTTP response alone is NOT a working site; examine redirects, DNS, TLS errors, title/body identity match, parked/expired/for-sale indicators, malware warning/security issues, maintenance/blank/host-default pages, broken navigation/contact, and material page content; handle anti-bot/Javascript uncertainty rather than wrongly calling dead. Use controlled DNS and HTTP probes with SSRF safeguards, optional browser render only for allowed pages. If site down but clearly historically owned, DO NOT call that company "no website". No website found after specified checks is a qualified inference, never absolute proof.
3. `email_status`: `LIKELY_DELIVERABLE`, `INVALID`, `CATCH_ALL`, `UNKNOWN`, `DISPOSABLE`, `DO_NOT_MAIL`, `UNVERIFIED`, with provider-native status/raw details, `checked_at`, expiration and verification method. Run formatting + domain/MX preliminary checks then (if configured) Hunter verification or ZeroBounce alternative; honor catch-all/greylisted/server-blocked cases. Don't perform intrusive arbitrary mailbox probes or send test messages to strangers. Even vendor-valid does not mean a human reads the inbox or that a named employee still works there. **Default Qualified export** excludes invalid, do-not-mail, disposable, unknown/catch-all/unverified by policy; allow clearly opt-in lower-assurance research export only if source rights permit, with explicit labels in the UI (default two-column CSV remains two columns). Never charge the user a qualified-lead credit for invalid/unverified results.
4. `contact_binding`: verify email comes from the exact company's website, verified official business social account, licensed contact dataset, or independently matched authorized directory. A domain's `hello@` address is high association only when that domain is confirmed the company's; listing-provider contact emails and other businesses' emails must never be incorrectly attributed. Public generic Gmail/Outlook addresses can be legitimate for website-less local businesses but need stronger direct evidence of ownership.
5. `freshness`: store source first/last seen, website checked time, email verified time, business activity reviewed time, and when exports were generated. Configurable default suggested age: email verification no older than 30 days, site check no older than 30 days, corroborating activity evidence no older than 90 days where available. These are internal policy targets, NOT guarantees. Recheck before export within budget; expire or warn when stale; preserve external provider's actual verification timestamp (which may be older than the API call).

**Quality gates**: Mark each result `QUALIFIED`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, `REJECTED`, or `PENDING_VERIFICATION`, with reasons. Default show/export Qualified; research view shows all statuses and causes. Missing evidence is not positive evidence. Count rejected businesses, inactive sites, mismatched emails, catch-all, unknown, verification failures, ambiguous identity, rights-limited results, and cost per qualified lead. Users can use filters for business activity, site health, email verification, freshness, confidence and source. Provide evidence drawer with 1-3 grounded URLs/dataset ids per assertion and last-check times. Show truthful "0 qualifying leads" rather than fake examples. Implement suppression + user flag business closed, bounced, wrong business, wrong email, opt out; use reports to avoid re-export and schedule optional revalidation. Never ignore feedback.

**Quality QA metrics**: measure percentage of qualified emails among discovered candidates; percentage matched to correct business; stale record rate; unknown status rate; duplicate rate; provider cost per Qualified lead; reported real-world bounce rate only from user-provided/authorized sending feedback. With reviewer-approved test dataset label truth and measure false-positive (claim operational when actually closed / wrong email attached) on sampled results. Do not manufacture rates or call an uncalibrated match score a true probability.

## Process rules

Pipeline:
1. Authenticated request, validate geography, niche, status, budget and provider selection.
2. Create SearchJob with stable idempotency key, job stages and reserved budget; dispatch durable event.
3. Query allowed imported business data / configured discovery provider(s) for selected geography and niche; save source references and source data rights.
4. Normalize and deduplicate, reject obviously mismatched regions/business names.
5. Investigate website candidates; status `HAS_WEBSITE` when matching owned business site is supported; `NO_WEBSITE_FOUND` if reasonable specified checks found none, with confidence/last-checked; otherwise `UNKNOWN` or `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
6. Email pathway A (HAS_WEBSITE): inspect public business site contact surfaces + optional Hunter under usage permissions. Pathway B (NO_WEBSITE_FOUND): Overture licensed contact fields and business social references, permitted listings, compliant web search for publicly listed emails, approved imports/partner API. No fabricated `name@gmail.com` guesses, no harvesting unrelated individuals.
7. Validate source->business association, deduplicate emails, optional verifier statuses.
8. Persist qualified leads and job progress, respect privacy/suppression, show evidence, handle partial failures and explicit 'no email found'.
9. Export exactly `Business Name,Email` by default, only licensed/exportable data; return accurate counts and reasons for skipped candidates.

Provide per-source provenance and `fieldRights` so a Google/partner-restricted business field cannot silently contaminate export records. Include a simple admin source-rights registry and licensing audit docs. Do not mix provider-restricted records into persistent output unless independently sourced from a permitted provider and independently matched.
